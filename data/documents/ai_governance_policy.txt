AI Governance Policy

Purpose
This policy establishes principles for the responsible development, deployment, and monitoring of artificial intelligence systems within the organization.

Model Approval
All high-impact AI systems must undergo a documented approval process before production deployment. The approval process must consider model purpose, data sources, expected users, potential risks, and applicable regulatory requirements.

Model Monitoring
Production AI systems must be monitored on an ongoing basis. Monitoring should cover model performance, data quality, significant changes in input distributions, operational failures, and other indicators of model degradation.

Human Oversight
High-impact decisions supported by AI must have appropriate human oversight. Employees must be able to review, challenge, and override AI-generated recommendations when necessary.

Incident Management
Material AI incidents must be documented, escalated, investigated, and reviewed. Corrective actions should be tracked until resolution.
