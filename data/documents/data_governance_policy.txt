Data Governance Policy

Purpose
This policy defines requirements for the secure, accurate, and accountable management of organizational data.

Data Quality
Critical business data should be complete, accurate, consistent, timely, and fit for its intended purpose. Data quality checks should be performed during collection, transformation, and reporting.

Data Access
Access to sensitive data must be granted according to business need and the principle of least privilege. Access rights should be reviewed periodically.

Data Lineage
Important datasets should have documented sources, transformations, owners, and downstream uses.

Data Retention
Data must be retained only for as long as required for legitimate business, legal, or regulatory purposes.
