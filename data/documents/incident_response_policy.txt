Incident Response Policy

Purpose
This policy defines the organization's approach to identifying, responding to, and recovering from technology and security incidents.

Identification
Potential incidents should be reported promptly through approved reporting channels.

Severity Assessment
Incidents should be classified according to business impact, affected systems, data sensitivity, and potential regulatory consequences.

Response
High-severity incidents require timely escalation to the responsible management and security teams. Response actions should be documented.

Post-Incident Review
Material incidents should undergo a post-incident review to identify root causes, control weaknesses, and corrective actions.
