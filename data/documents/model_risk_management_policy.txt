Model Risk Management Policy

Purpose
This policy establishes requirements for identifying, assessing, and managing risks associated with analytical and machine learning models.

Model Validation
Models used for material business decisions should undergo independent or appropriately controlled validation before deployment.

Performance Testing
Model performance should be tested using representative validation data and relevant performance metrics.

Change Management
Material model changes must be documented and reviewed before deployment.

Ongoing Review
Models should be periodically reassessed to identify performance deterioration, data drift, unexpected behavior, or other emerging risks.
