Access Control Policy

Purpose
This policy establishes controls for protecting systems and information from unauthorized access.

Least Privilege
Users and services should receive only the permissions required to perform their assigned responsibilities.

Authentication
Privileged systems must use strong authentication controls. Multi-factor authentication should be enabled where supported.

Access Review
User access to critical systems must be reviewed regularly. Unnecessary or excessive permissions must be removed promptly.

Privileged Access
Privileged activities should be logged and monitored. Shared privileged accounts should be avoided unless explicitly approved.
